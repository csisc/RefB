Wikidata PubMed referencer

Finds Wikidata statements between two items that have no reference, searches PubMed for papers mentioning both items, and shows the sentences that support the claim. Pick a paper, then export a QuickStatements batch.

1. Choose items
2. QuickStatements batch
Open QuickStatements

Each line adds a reference to the existing statement: PubMed ID (S698) and retrieval date (S813). Review every paper before running the batch.

Data from Wikidata (CC0) and PubMed via NCBI E-utilities. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to any server of ours.
